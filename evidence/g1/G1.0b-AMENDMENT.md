Base HEAD: `2125deef99255c5396fd2919ceb82c56ad54520b`

This amendment does NOT rewrite G1.0. G1.0 remains historically
recorded as:

```text
fresh selection = STOP
reason = VISUAL group had 1 candidate < 2 required
runtime changes = 0
```

`evidence/g1/selection.json` is preserved unchanged as the record of
that STOP. `evidence/g1/selection-v2.json` becomes the canonical
selection for G1.1/G1.2.

## Amendment

The original `2 TEXT + 2 VISUAL` design is impossible under the frozen
G0-G.0 index and the G1 semantic seen-set: exactly 3 fresh candidates
satisfy `declared_modifying_instrument_count >= 2`.

The holdout requirement is replaced by a **census**:

```text
SEALED_HOLDOUT = all fresh semantically-unseen candidates satisfying
                 declared_modifying_instrument_count >= 2
```

Deterministic result (no ranking, no human selection):

| target | group | modifying instruments | anejo refs |
|---|---|---|---|
| BOE-A-2014-1183 | TEXT | 4 | 0 |
| BOE-A-2010-15521 | TEXT | 2 | 0 |
| BOE-A-2012-9058 | VISUAL | 6 | 6 |

The amendment-density threshold is NOT lowered; shallow one-modifier
candidates are NOT admitted to balance strata.

the integrity verifier may read bytes for hashes. The future G1.2
evaluator may access them only after its own pre-open freeze.
